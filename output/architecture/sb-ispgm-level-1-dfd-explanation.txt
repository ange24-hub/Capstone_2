Level 1 Data Flow Diagram of the Developed System

The diagram expands the system's context diagram into five main processes and shows how information moves between residents, barangay secretaries, Municipal DILG users, and the system's data stores. Rectangles represent external entities, rounded rectangles represent processes, parallel lines represent logical data stores, and labeled arrows indicate the direction and content of each data flow. Repeated entity and data-store identifiers refer to the same users and records; they are repeated to avoid crossing arrows.

Process 1.0, Manage Accounts and Approvals, receives registration and login details from users and secretary approval decisions from the Municipal DILG user. It reads and updates D1, Accounts and Approvals, and returns account status, approval status, and secretary applications to the appropriate users. Process 2.0, Manage Residents, RBI and Migration Records, receives household and resident information, RBI submissions, and migration events from barangay secretaries. It also accepts barangay configuration from authorized Municipal DILG users. Validated information is stored in D2, Registry and Migration Records, and retrieved for record viewing and updates.

Process 3.0, Process Document Requests and Payment Review, receives document requests and payment evidence from residents. Barangay secretaries review these submissions and provide processing decisions. The process stores requests, payment records, decisions, and related files in D3, then returns request status and payment instructions to residents and pending transactions to staff. Payment evidence supports staff verification; submitting a receipt does not itself transfer money. Process 4.0, Manage Concerns and Replies, handles resident concerns, attachments, staff responses, and status updates. It uses D4 to maintain the conversation history and return updates to the appropriate users.

Process 5.0, Generate Reports, Maps and Migration Forecasts, receives selection criteria from barangay secretaries and Municipal DILG users. It retrieves registry, geographic, and verified migration information from D2, service transaction records from D3, and concern records from D4. These inputs support barangay reports, RBI reports, municipal summaries, maps, and migration estimates according to each user's access permissions. Migration forecasts require sufficient verified monthly history; transaction and concern records support summaries rather than serving as migration forecasting inputs.

The diagram presents the core business flows. Shared authentication checks, activity logging, and email transport details are omitted to keep it readable. The logical stores group related database records and protected files and do not imply that each store is a separate physical database.
